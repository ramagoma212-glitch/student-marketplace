Student Market Place – Project Overview

Student Market Place is a digital platform designed to allow students to buy, sell and exchange goods and services within their institution.

    Problem & solution Main features How the system works Benefits Financial overview Research Prototype analytics Project team
    The problem

    Essentials are expensive – and unused items go to waste

    Students often struggle with the high cost of textbooks, laptops, clothing, furniture and other essential items, while other students own unused items they could sell. General marketplaces are not designed around student communities, which makes it hard to trust buyers and sellers and to find local deals on campus.

    Proposed solution

    A student-only, campus-focused marketplace

    A website where students register with their TUT student email, list products or services, browse and search listings, message sellers directly, confirm purchases and review sellers. Categories include textbooks, electronics, accommodation, clothing and student services.

        About this prototype

        This website is the web prototype component of the project, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript only. Data is stored in the browser (localStorage) and no real payments or email verification take place. The project's Java implementation phase is a separate deliverable. Student Market Place is not an official service of, or endorsed by, Tshwane University of Technology.
