Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before buying or selling on Student Market Place.

1. Who may use the marketplace

Student Market Place is only for registered students of Tshwane University of Technology. You must register with your official @tut4life.ac.za student email address and keep your login details private.

2. Responsibility for transactions

  • Users are responsible for their own transactions. Student Market Place only connects buyers and sellers.
  • Buyer and seller must agree on the price, payment method and collection point themselves.
  • Users should verify products before payment – inspect the item in person and make sure it matches the listing.
  • Avoid sending money before confirming the seller and the item.

3. Prohibited listings

Prohibited or illegal products may not be listed. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco or any illegal substances
  • Stolen, counterfeit or pirated goods (including copied software or exam papers)
  • Academic dishonesty services such as writing assignments or tests for others
  • Adult content or anything offensive or discriminatory

4. Respectful behaviour

Users must behave respectfully. Harassment, threats, hate speech, spam or scams will result in removal from the platform. Keep communication inside the marketplace inbox wherever possible.

5. Reporting

Fake listings, scams, inappropriate products or incorrect information can be reported using the Report listing button on any item. Reports are reviewed by the platform team.

6. Ratings and reviews

Reviews must be honest and based on a real transaction. Ratings range from 1 to 5 stars and are shown publicly on seller profiles.

7. Privacy

Your email address and student number are never shown publicly. Other students only see your first name, surname initial, campus, listings and reviews.
