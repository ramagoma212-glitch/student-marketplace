A trusted marketplace made for TUT students

Student Market Place is a digital platform designed to allow students to buy, sell and exchange goods and services within their institution.

Our aims

What the platform sets out to achieve.

The problem

Many students struggle to find affordable textbooks, laptops, clothing, furniture and other essentials. At the same time, other students have items they no longer use but have no simple and safe way to sell them. Existing online marketplaces are not designed specifically for students, which makes it difficult to trust buyers and sellers.

Our solution

A website where verified students create accounts, list products or services, browse items, contact sellers through an internal inbox and complete transactions safely. Only registered students with an official TUT email can join, creating a trusted environment supported by ratings, reviews and reporting.
